The kitchen

A working pantry, not a museum brand.

Pandey's Real Taste started as jars the family sent to cousins who missed homemade achar. The same logic now applies to masala and staples: buy well, pack clean, price like a kirana.

Pickles are cured in food-grade jars with mustard oil. Spices are roasted in small kadhais and milled the same week they ship. Rice, dal and atta come from mills we can name.
